# Sport Sport Sport - deal sources

The daily scan works through this list every weekday. Search it with `site:` queries for the last 24-48 hours
(e.g. `site:sportspro.com partnership`), plus plain-language searches like "named official partner", "signs
sponsorship", "naming rights", "jersey patch", "front-of-shirt" and "extends partnership".

## Accuracy rules

1. **Confirmed only.** A deal counts when the brand, the rights holder, or a reputable outlet reports it as
   signed. Rumours, "in talks" and "set to" stories are skipped.
2. **Official first.** Where possible, confirm on the rights holder's or brand's own newsroom (Tier 1).
3. **One official source is enough.** A deal announced only on the club's, league's or brand's own website or official
   social account is confirmed. Most small deals only ever have that one source, and that's fine: log it.
4. **Two sources for anything big.** If a figure, length or "record/first" claim is used, it must appear in an
   official release or in two independent outlets. If not, leave the figure out.
5. **Date = announcement date**, not the date a story was republished.
6. **Brand first, then the property** in every deal name.

## Using this list
- Run at least one search per Tier 1 and Tier 2 group each morning.
- ANZ deals: always check the AFL, NRL, Cricket Australia, Football Australia and Tennis Australia newsrooms,
  plus B&T, mi-3 and Mumbrella.
- Many ANZ club deals are announced only on club websites or social posts. Check club newsrooms directly, not just
  search results, and accept an official club social post as Tier 1.
- If a deal appears only in Tier 3 or 4, look for a Tier 1 or Tier 2 confirmation before using it.

## Standing run rules (added 22 Oct 2026, override the skill where they differ)

### 1. Open these pages first, every run
Web search barely indexes the last few days, so it's a backstop only. Before any open search, open each of
these pages directly and work through every deal dated inside the window:
- B&T sports marketing: https://www.bandt.com.au/category/sports-marketing/ (plus the Spotlight on Sponsors column)
- Sponsorship News: https://www.sponsorshipnews.com.au/index.php?stream=1-3-7
- Ministry of Sport sponsorship feed: https://ministryofsport.com/topic/sponsorship/
- SportsPro The Wire: https://www.sportspro.com/the-wire/ (last 24 hours and last week sections)
- Sportcal latest: https://www.sportcal.com/region/europe/spain/ (its latest-news list is site-wide)
- Tennis Australia media releases, AFL, NRL, Cricket Australia and Football Australia newsrooms
Then check the ANZ club newsrooms and run the open-web sweeps.

### 2. Never say who a brand replaces unless the official release says so
Trade press gets predecessors wrong (B&T said Hahn replaced Coopers at the AO; Coopers left in 2019).
A "replaces X" or "takes over from X" line needs the rights holder's or brand's own release, or it stays out.

### 3. LinkedIn deliverable format
Send Angus the post, then a "Today's deals" block with one short snippet for every deal on the graphics
(lead included), in the style of Notice Sport's weekly round-up:
- One sentence each, starting with the property or brand: "[Property] named [Brand] as ..." or
  "[Brand] signed on as ...", then " - " and one line of the most interesting verified detail.
- Put @ in front of every brand and property name so Angus can tag them as he pastes
  (e.g. "@Tennis Australia named @Lion's Hahn the Official Beer of ...").
- Verified facts only, same voice rules as the post. No figures or lengths that failed the accuracy gate.
- End with: "Every deal, filed: sportsportsport.com.au"
- Angus tags the pages himself, so don't look up or list LinkedIn page URLs.

### 4. No Instagram stories or Reels
Switched off for good (22 Oct 2026). Queue files carry the feed carousel and caption only: no "stories",
no "reel_caption", no "story_time". The renderer and poster skip both.

### 5. One photo everywhere, free Unsplash only (2 Nov 2026)
The lead's deals.json "image" is the single photo for the website, LinkedIn and Instagram.
- It must be a good-quality, free Unsplash photo (https://images.unsplash.com/photo-..., never Unsplash+),
  with "imageCredit": "Unsplash". Never a club, brand or press photo, even with credit.
- Any photo swap goes into deals.json "image" first (then re-run tools/build_articles.py). Never change the
  photo in instagram/linkedin/<date>.json or a queue file alone: the LinkedIn spec "image" must always equal
  the deals.json "image".
- Before queuing Instagram, check the two match. If the LinkedIn spec has an Unsplash photo and deals.json
  doesn't, Angus picked the LinkedIn one: copy it into deals.json.

### 6. Weekly schedule (from 3 Nov 2026)
SSS no longer posts deals every weekday. The week is:
- Monday: Feature (website article, LinkedIn post + graphics, Instagram carousel). Task: "Sport Sport Sport Monday feature".
- Tuesday: Deals. Task: "Sport Sport Sport deals (Tue/Thu)".
- Wednesday: Data drop - one insight built from our own archive (deals.json, coverage.json, partners and properties data). Task: "Sport Sport Sport Wednesday data drop".
- Thursday: Deals. Same task as Tuesday.
- Friday: Deal breakdown - the week's best deal unpacked in depth. Task: "Sport Sport Sport Friday breakdown".
- Weekends: nothing posts.
Deal run windows: Tuesday covers everything announced since the Thursday run (the last 5 days, Fri to Tue); Thursday
covers the last 3 days (Tue to Thu). Wherever a prompt or the skill says "last 72 hours", "last 48 hours" or "last 24
hours", use that window instead, for logging, the lead and the round-up. The lead should be the strongest confirmed deal
in the window, favouring the freshest when two are close.
Monday, Wednesday and Friday posts are features (full steps in FEATURES.md): each one is an entry in features.json with "label" set to "Feature",
"Data drop" or "Breakdown", built with tools/build_articles.py, plus an Instagram queue file with "kind": "feature" (slide 1
pulls the photo from features.json) and a LinkedIn spec in instagram/linkedin/<date>.json (slide 2 heading set with
"label"). Instagram posts Mon-Thu 7pm and Fri 6pm. The Tuesday evergreen and Friday "week in deals" carousels are
retired. The Friday breakdown message carries the "Instagram this week" stats.
Never write wording (site, posts, captions) that promises daily or every-weekday posting.

### 7. Thorough scan on Tue/Thu (from 3 Nov 2026, replaces usage-light mode)
With deals down to two runs a week, each run is thorough. The usage-light limits are gone.
- Open every page in rule 1 directly, plus the Tennis Australia, AFL, NRL, Cricket Australia, Football Australia,
  Rugby Australia, Netball Australia, NBL, NZ Rugby and NZ Cricket newsrooms (reach each through WebSearch results).
- Then check the ANZ club newsrooms that are most active (AFL and NRL clubs first, then A-League, BBL/WBBL, NBL,
  Super Netball, Super Rugby), through WebSearch results.
- Then 10-15 open-web backstop searches across the whole window (site: queries plus "official partner",
  "signs sponsorship", "naming rights", "jersey patch", "extends partnership", "multi-year deal", "principal partner").
- Window: Tuesday = the last 5 days (since Thursday's run), Thursday = the last 3 days. Nothing older is logged.
- Accuracy gate: the verification subagent checks the lead AND every round-up deal in the post (not just the lead).
  Anything that fails is dropped from the post. Everything else logged to deals.json still needs its source page opened
  in the run, both parties and deal type matching, and the announcement date confirmed.
