Equity in her own tee.

Rugby Bricks × Sophie de Goede

Rugby unionSignature productANZ

A New Zealand kicking-tee brand just gave a Canadian rugby captain equity in her own gear.

Rugby Bricks has signed World Player of the Year Sophie de Goede for the first signature kicking tee in women's rugby. The RB Vortex is co-designed by de Goede, with a red colourway for her Canadian heritage, and she takes 20% of every unit sold.

Signature product deals with revenue share are common in men's sport (boots, bats, rackets) but rare in women's rugby, where sponsorship infrastructure is still catching up. A boutique brand backing a genuine star with real upside, rather than chasing a marquee logo deal, builds credibility fast.

More equipment brands will start trading flat fees for equity in the athletes wearing their gear.
