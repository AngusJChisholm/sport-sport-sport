One patch. Eighteen sports.

Kin Insurance × FSU Athletics

College sportJersey patchGlobal
Photo: Unsplash

Kin Insurance just put its logo on every jersey across Florida State's athletic department: all 18 sports, one deal.

The insurer becomes the first brand ever to appear as a jersey patch across FSU's full sporting program, in a four-year agreement that also makes it the school's preferred insurance partner. It debuted on the football jersey against SMU.

Most jersey-patch deals stay locked to the marquee sport, where the cameras are. Buying across an entire athletic department trades spotlight exposure for blanket presence: every roster, and every recruiting pitch the school makes.

Expect more brands to buy the whole department instead of just the biggest sport.
