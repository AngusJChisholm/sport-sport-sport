Woolworths stocks up on rugby.

Woolworths × Rugby World Cup 2027

Rugby unionOfficial supporterANZ

Woolworths has put its hand up for both home Rugby World Cups.

Woolworths has been named the Official Supermarket Supporter of the men's Rugby World Cup 2027 and the women's Rugby World Cup 2029, both being played in Australia. The deal was announced on Tuesday 20 October. Neither side has put a number or an end date on it beyond the two tournaments, so we won't either.

It slots Woolworths into World Rugby's Official Suppliers and Supporters tier, alongside names like Dove Men+Care, Gilbert, Macron and Mitsubishi Estate. Above that sit the official partners (Liqui Moly, Coca-Cola, IHG) and the principal partners, Emirates, Asahi Super Dry and Visa.

The interesting bit is the retail media angle. Woolworths says its Cartology network will open up brand opportunities around the tournaments. That turns a supermarket sponsorship into something bigger: a channel other brands can buy into, with the World Cup as the hook and the checkout as the payoff. Woolworths reckons it serves around 24 million customers a week, which is a lot of eyeballs for a pack of snags or a slab of something cold.

The men's tournament runs from 1 October to 13 November 2027, with 24 teams across seven host cities. Woolworths is pitching itself as a one-stop shop for fans on both sides of the Tasman, with fans in New Zealand part of the pitch as much as fans here.

What to watch: how hard Woolworths pushes Cartology deals off the back of this, and whether the other big grocers or liquor chains try to muscle in around the edges with their own rugby plays.

In a home World Cup, the supermarket aisle might be the best seat in the house.
