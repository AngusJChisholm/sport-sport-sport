Mbappé takes equity.

On × Kylian Mbappé

FootballAthlete endorsementGlobal

Kylian Mbappé just walked away from Nike after 20 years, and picked equity over a bigger cheque.

He's signed with Swiss brand On, taking equity in the business instead of a standard endorsement fee. On will build its first football boots in 2027, and has already added Thierry Henry as director of football.

The equity angle matters more than the boots. On is betting that owning part of a challenger brand beats another decade of royalty cheques from the market leader, and Mbappé's happy to bet alongside them. Federer proved the model works in tennis, and On is running the same play in football.

Expect more superstars to ask for equity over a signing bonus.

Boot deals used to be about who pays the most. Now the smart ones are about who owns the most.
