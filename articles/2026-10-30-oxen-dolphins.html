OXEN dresses the Dolphins.

OXEN × Dolphins

NRLKit supplierANZ
Photo: Supplied

A UK sportswear brand has landed one of the NRL's newest clubs, and the Dolphins will play in OXEN from 2027.

The Dolphins have named OXEN as their On-Field Apparel Partner from 2027. The club's own announcement also calls the brand its Official Technical and Kit Partner, and the deal covers supporter merchandise as well as the playing kit. No length or value has been disclosed.

OXEN is a UK-founded performance sportswear and lifestyle brand, and it already knows the club. It has already backed the Dolphins Academy and been a boot partner for the club, so this is a step up from the Academy and the boots to the main jersey. The new ECHO home jersey was unveiled alongside the announcement.

The club's chief executive Terry Reader said OXEN has been a valued partner and the club is keen to take the relationship to the next level. OXEN Sports chief executive Lee Jenkinson said the brand was proud to be joining the Dolphins at such an exciting stage in the club's journey.

OXEN has also been building a local footprint. The announcement says the company has invested in an Australian headquarters and distribution centre, which matters when you are supplying a club's merchandise as well as its match kit. It is also already in the NRL ecosystem as the Parramatta Eels' footwear partner, a deal announced in 2025.

Why it matters: a challenger brand is taking on a whole NRL club, playing kit and supporter range included, rather than just boots. For the Dolphins it means a supplier that is already invested locally and that has plenty to prove, and for OXEN it puts its product in front of a full NRL crowd.

OXEN has moved from the boots to the jersey, and the Dolphins are the test case.
