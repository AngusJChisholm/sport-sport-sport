Monaco's shirt for Europe.

Suisscourtage Assurances × AS Monaco

FootballMajor partnerGlobal
Photo: Unsplash

Monaco has signed a shirt sponsor just for its European nights.

AS Monaco has named Suisscourtage Assurances its new major partner for the UEFA Conference League. The insurance broker's name goes on the front of the first-team shirt for European matches this season, and it picks up LED advertising at home games at Stade Louis-II. No fee or length beyond the coming season has been put out. The campaign starts away to CSKA Sofia on Thursday 15 October.

It's a local deal through and through. Suisscourtage was founded in Monaco in 1988, sits inside the JV Pastor Group and employs 220 people, working with private clients and businesses that need cover for complex risks and high-value assets. Monaco CEO Thiago Scuro pitched it as two Monegasque organisations joining forces, and that's the point: a club from a tiny principality putting a home-grown business on the shirt when it travels.

The timing tells the story. Only last week Monaco signed Mammouth AI as a premium partner through 2029, with the AI company appearing on the shirt in Ligue 1. This is Monaco's first go at the Conference League, so rather than bundle Europe into an existing deal, the club treated it as fresh inventory and sold it separately.

That's the bit worth watching across the game. Competition-specific shirt deals let clubs squeeze more out of the same piece of fabric, and they open the door to brands that can't stretch to a full-season domestic deal but can afford a European run. For a broker chasing wealthy clients, a handful of televised European nights with a club tied to Monaco is a tidy fit.

What to watch next: how far Monaco go in the competition, because a deeper run means more matches and more value for Suisscourtage, and whether the deal rolls over if Monaco land back in the Champions League.

Expect more clubs to sell the shirt one competition at a time.
