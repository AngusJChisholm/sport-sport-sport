ARN books five summers.

ARN × Australian Open

TennisAudio partnerANZ
Photo: Unsplash

ARN has locked in the Australian Open for the next five years.

ARN and Tennis Australia have signed a five-year partnership that starts with AO27, announced at ARN's Upfront27 event this week. ARN's metro talent will broadcast live from Melbourne Park across the tournament, and the package goes well past the shows themselves: a dedicated Australian Open DAB+ channel in Sydney and Melbourne, live streaming on iHeart, podcasts, YouTube and social content, regional network coverage, and an Are Media extension that brings ELLE and Gourmet Traveller into the mix for advertisers. Neither side has put a number on it.

This isn't ARN's first summer at Melbourne Park. It was the AO's official audio partner in 2025, and this new term pushes the relationship out a long way further. ARN's chief content officer, Kerri Elstub, didn't undersell it: "This is a huge commitment and a major step up from where we've been."

The interesting bit is the shape. Audio partners used to mean a few live crosses and some branded promos. This reads more like ARN treating the AO as a three-week content tentpole: on-air, on a channel of its own, on streaming and in magazines, all pointed at one event every January. For a radio network trying to sell advertisers a summer story, owning that much of the biggest event on the calendar is the pitch.

For Tennis Australia it's another long-term partner, and one that reaches fans in the car, at work and on the train, not just in front of a screen. Five years also gives both sides time to build the AO channel into something listeners actually seek out rather than a one-summer experiment.

What to watch: what actually runs on the AO DAB+ channel in January, how ARN sells the Are Media add-on to brands, and whether other big Australian events start packaging audio rights the same way.

The AO's summer soundtrack is sorted for the next five Januarys.
