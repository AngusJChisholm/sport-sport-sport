Yoghurt takes the WTA Finals.

FAGE × WTA Finals

TennisTitle sponsorshipGlobal
Photo: Unsplash

A yoghurt brand now has its name on the biggest week in women's tennis.

FAGE has been named title partner of the WTA Finals in a multi-year deal that runs through 2029. It also becomes the official yoghurt partner of the WTA. The WTA announced it on Tuesday 20 October, US time. No fee has been disclosed, so we won't guess at one.

The timing is the story. The Finals are on the move: this year's edition heads to Indian Wells, California, from 8 to 15 November, and from 2027 the event settles in Charlotte, North Carolina, for three editions. FAGE's name sits across all four: one stopover in the desert and three in the new home.

That matters for the WTA. Moving a flagship event is a commercial risk, and a title partner locked in before the first ball in Charlotte gives the tour something to build the rest of its sales around. The deal was brokered by THE·TEAM's US rights sales group working with SPORTFIVE, and the rights cover broadcast and venue branding, sampling, fan experiences and hospitality, with year-round marketing planned from 2027.

For FAGE it's a clean fit. Women's tennis skews toward the health-conscious, well-off shopper that a premium yoghurt brand chases in supermarket aisles, and a title deal puts the brand in every broadcast mention of the event rather than on a courtside board.

What to watch: who fills the rest of the Charlotte partner roster, and whether more food and drink brands follow FAGE into title rights across women's sport. If the US move lands well, the next round of WTA deals should get easier to sell.

The Finals have a new home and a new name to go with it.
