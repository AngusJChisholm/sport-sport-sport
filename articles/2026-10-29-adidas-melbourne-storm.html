Storm go three stripes.

adidas × Melbourne Storm

Rugby leagueApparel partnerANZ
Photo: Mumbrella

adidas is the Melbourne Storm's new Official Apparel Partner, and the three stripes will make their debut in Las Vegas.

Melbourne Storm have named adidas as their Official Apparel Partner on a multi-year agreement. The partnership runs through adidas Pacific and covers playing kit, training, performance and lifestyle apparel for the NRL squad, the club's elite male and female pathway teams and its academy programs. Neither side has put a number on it or said how long it lasts.

The timing does a lot of the work. The Storm's new jersey is set to debut when the club opens its 2027 season in Las Vegas, a first for the club on US soil. Storm executive Tayah Bot says the club will be debuting the new jersey on the world stage. For a global sportswear brand, that is a made-for-TV launch moment with an American audience watching an NRL strip for the first time.

It also shows how far the apparel conversation has moved. A kit partnership used to be about the jersey and the merch rack. This one spans the pathways, the women's program and the academy, which means one brand shapes how the club looks from junior representative level up. adidas Pacific's Rachel Howard is on the record backing the club's elite programs, which tells you where the brand sees the value.

For the NRL, it is another sign that the big global sportswear names see rugby league as worth a serious play, particularly when the schedule gives them a stage like Vegas. For rivals, it raises the bar on what an apparel partner is expected to bring beyond supplying kit.

What to watch next: the jersey reveal itself, how adidas uses the Vegas trip in its global marketing, and whether the retail range lands before or after the big night.

The Storm have picked their moment. Vegas is the runway.
