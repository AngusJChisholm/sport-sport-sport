adidas names the academy.

adidas × Benfica

FootballKit extension + naming rightsGlobal

adidas just bought naming rights to the academy that produced Bernardo Silva, João Félix and Rúben Dias.

adidas has extended its kit deal with Benfica through 2033, reported at €120 million and the biggest sponsorship in Portuguese football history. It stretches a relationship that goes back to 1997, and the new terms hand adidas naming rights to Benfica's Seixal training academy, which becomes the adidas Benfica Campus.

Kit deals usually buy visibility on match day. This one buys adidas a permanent presence inside the pipeline that actually produces the players, turning a facility into brand real estate instead of just matchday exposure.

Once every major stadium already has a naming partner, training grounds look like the next thing clubs can sell.

Sponsorship money is starting to chase where players are made, not just where they're worn.
