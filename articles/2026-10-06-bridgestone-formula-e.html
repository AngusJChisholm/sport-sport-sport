Bridgestone goes electric.

Bridgestone × Formula E

MotorsportTyre supplier + title roundsGlobal
Photo: Unsplash

Bridgestone is walking back into top-flight motorsport, this time as both tyre supplier and title sponsor.

The Japanese giant has signed on as Formula E's sole tyre supplier from the 2026/27 season, taking title billing on four marquee rounds including Mexico City and Tokyo. The new tyres are built from 65% recycled and renewable material, launching alongside the championship's next-gen GEN4 car.

A legacy tyre brand betting big on electric racing says plenty about where motorsport sponsorship money is heading. Pairing the comeback with a real sustainability figure, rather than just a logo on the sidewall, gives Bridgestone a story to tell well beyond the partnership itself.

Expect more legacy automotive brands chasing the same electric-racing halo in seasons to come.
