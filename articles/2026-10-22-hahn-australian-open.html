Hahn pours into the AO.

Hahn × Australian Open

TennisOfficial partnerANZ
Photo: Tennis Australia

Hahn has landed the biggest pour of the Australian summer.

Tennis Australia has named Lion's Hahn the Official Beer of the Australian Open and the Australian Summer of Tennis in a multi-year deal announced on Thursday 22 October. Neither side has put a length or a number on it, so we won't either.

The centrepiece is Hahn House, a two-storey venue with a beer garden on Grand Slam Oval, which Tennis Australia is pitching as a new social hub for the tournament. Lion is bringing more of its portfolio too: Stone & Wood takes over Garden Square, and Four Pillars runs a new gin bar at Kia Arena.

Away from Melbourne Park, Hahn plans limited-edition packaging, competitions, tennis merch and surprise experiences, plus pop-up pickleball courts and racket-shaped drinks trays around the country. That reach matters. The AO is a fortnight, but the Summer of Tennis gives Hahn a whole season of pub and bottle-shop activity to hang it on.

It also rounds out a big sporting slate for the brand. Hahn already holds the naming rights to the Hahn Australia Cup and is the official beer of the Wallabies, so the AO gives Lion a marquee summer property to sit alongside its football and rugby plays.

What to watch: how Hahn House lands with AO crowds in January, and whether Lion stacks more of its brands across the Summer of Tennis events outside Melbourne.
