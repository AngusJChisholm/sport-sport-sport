La Liga deals itself in.

LIGAS Gaming × La Liga

FootballLicensingGlobal

La Liga has just become a real-money gambling operator.

Spain's top football league has signed an exclusive global licensing deal with B2B studio LIGAS Gaming, letting it build and distribute real-money football games under the La Liga brand across regulated markets outside Spain.

That's a real shift in how a league makes money from gambling. Rather than collecting ad dollars from a betting sponsor, La Liga now earns directly off every wager placed inside its own branded product.

With betting-ad rules tightening across multiple markets, licensing the IP directly lets a rights holder keep the upside while sidestepping some of that scrutiny.

Expect more leagues to start treating real-money gaming as a product they own outright, not just an ad category they sell into.
