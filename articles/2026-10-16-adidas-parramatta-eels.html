Three stripes for Parra.

adidas × Parramatta Eels

NRLApparel partnerANZ
Photo: Unsplash

adidas is putting the Parramatta Eels in three stripes.

The Parramatta Eels have signed a multi-year deal that makes adidas the club's official apparel partner. It covers a lot of ground: the NRL and NRLW teams, the club's elite academy programs and its junior representative sides. Both top teams will run out in adidas for the first time in Round 1 of the 2027 season. Neither side has put out a length or a dollar figure.

The club launched it with a campaign called "Made for Parra", and the kit and retail range are due to be unveiled at a launch later this year. That reveal is where the fans get their first proper look at what three stripes in blue and gold actually means.

Why it matters: apparel is one of the few deals that touches every part of a club. Sponsors get a logo on a jersey. A kit supplier gets the jersey itself, the training gear, the merch store and every junior player pulling on a Parra top on a Saturday morning. Signing the pathways as well as first grade means adidas is in front of the next generation of Eels fans years before they buy a membership.

It's also a nice get for adidas, a brand most Aussie fans link with football and rugby union rather than the NRL. Landing the Eels in Western Sydney, with a deal that runs right down to the juniors, gives it a real foothold in rugby league heartland.

What to watch next: the kit reveal and how hard adidas pushes the retail side, and whether the brand follows up with more rugby league clubs once the Eels range is on the shelves.

When a global brand dresses the juniors too, it's buying the next generation of fans.
