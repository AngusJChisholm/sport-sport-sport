GWM rolls into Parra.

GWM × Parramatta Eels

NRLSleeve sponsorshipANZ
Photo: Supplied

GWM wants to be Australia's number five car brand, and it's driving through Parramatta to get there.

The Parramatta Eels have signed GWM as their official automotive partner on a multi-year deal that kicks off in the 2027 season. The car maker also takes the sleeve on both the NRL and NRLW playing jerseys, so the same badge runs across the men's and women's programs. Neither side has put a number on it.

It's been a big week in the Eels' commercial office. Two days earlier the club confirmed adidas as its new apparel partner across the NRL and NRLW sides, with the new kit due in Round 1 next year. So by the time Parra runs out in 2027 the jersey, and what's printed on the arm, will both be new.

For GWM this is part of a pattern. It's already the official automotive partner of the NZ Warriors and a joint major partner at Richmond in the AFL. GWM's Australia and New Zealand chief operating officer John Kett was blunt about the goal: become the country's number five automotive brand. He also had a crack at the club's ambitions, saying that for Parramatta the goal is premiership number five.

The logic is easy to follow. New South Wales is the biggest car market in the country and western Sydney is a heartland of rugby league, so a sleeve at Parra puts the brand in front of a huge chunk of potential buyers every week. Taking the asset across both the NRL and NRLW kits also squeezes more airtime out of one spot on the shirt, and gives GWM a presence in the women's game without a separate deal.

What to watch next: how GWM activates it. Car brands in footy usually lean on vehicle fleets, member offers and dealer tie-ins, so expect GWM to show up well beyond the sleeve. The other thing to watch is whether GWM keeps adding clubs. Two codes and three big teams in, it's starting to look like a portfolio rather than a one-off.

New kit, new car partner, same week. Not a bad start to 2027 for the Eels.
