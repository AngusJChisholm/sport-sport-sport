Quest moves to the front.

Quest Apartment Hotels × Cronulla Sharks

NRLMajor partnerANZ
Photo: Cronulla Sharks

Quest Apartment Hotels started on the Sharks' NRLW jersey. From 2027 it's on the front of the men's one too.

Quest Apartment Hotels has signed a three-year extension with the Cronulla Sharks that makes it the club's front-of-jersey major partner for the NRL side from the 2027 season. The club calls it a Premier Major Partner role. Quest isn't walking away from the women either: it stays on with the NRLW team, so the brand will sit on both Sharks jerseys.

The relationship isn't new. Quest came on board in 2024, backing the club's membership program and the NRLW side. Two years on, it has stepped up to the front of the men's jersey. David Mansfield, Managing Director of The Ascott Limited, Australasia, put it this way: "We're particularly proud to build on our support of the Sharks' women's team and now stand alongside both the men's and women's teams as a major partner."

That path is the bit worth noting. Women's sport keeps pitching itself to brands as a smart entry point: get in early, build the relationship, prove the fit. Quest is a working example of how that can play out in rugby league. It backed the NRLW team and the members first, then moved up to the main jersey with the same club, rather than arriving cold with a one-off front-of-jersey cheque.

It's a tidy fit on paper too. An accommodation brand gets a natural story with a club whose teams and travelling fans are on the road most weekends, and members who already know the name from the membership program. The Sharks get a partner that has already shown it will put money into more than one part of the club.

What to watch: how Quest uses the jersey in 2027, whether the NRLW and NRL assets are sold together in its activation, and whether other NRL clubs start pointing brands at the women's jersey as the way in.

The NRLW partnership was the audition. Quest got the part.
