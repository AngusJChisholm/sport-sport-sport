Dressed for the walk in.

CONNOR × Sydney FC

FootballOfficial off-field style partnerANZ
Photo: Sydney FC

Sydney FC has sold the walk from the team bus to the sheds.

CONNOR has signed a two-year partnership with Sydney FC as the club's Official Off-Field Style Partner. The Australian menswear chain will supply suiting and fashion outfits for the first team, coaching staff and club officials, covering matchday arrivals and club functions. The partnership also takes in matchday activations, player appearances, in-store experiences and offers for members.

The split in the wardrobe is the interesting part. Under Armour stays as the club's on-field apparel partner, so CONNOR is buying a different moment: what the players wear before and after the whistle. Football clubs have long had a formalwear line in the commercial deck, but the arrival has become a piece of content in its own right, and that gives a tailored-clothing brand something it can actually use.

The fit between brand and fans is the pitch. CONNOR has more than 200 stores across Australia and New Zealand, and its general manager, Marc Miller, described its customer as someone who is at the game on the weekend and following his team all season. Matt Pound, Sydney FC General Manager Commercial and Marketing, made the same point from the club's side: "Importantly, this partnership goes beyond outfitting the team."

For a mid-market retailer, that is a neat piece of category thinking. A store network means the partnership has somewhere to land beyond a logo on a backdrop, and player appearances give the shops a reason to shout about it. Sydney FC's commercial team, meanwhile, gets a new line item that doesn't tread on its kit partner.

The thing to watch is how much of this turns up where fans actually see it. If the arrivals become regular content and the in-store offers feel like more than a discount code, expect other A-League clubs to go looking for their own off-field partner.

The kit partnership gets the logo. This one gets the arrival shot.
