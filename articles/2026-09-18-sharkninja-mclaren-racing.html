Kitchen gear meets the pit lane.

SharkNinja × McLaren Racing

MotorsportOfficial partnerGlobal

McLaren Racing just signed a kitchen appliance brand as an official partner, and the deal goes well past logos on the car.

SharkNinja is now a multi-year partner across McLaren's Formula 1, Arrow McLaren IndyCar, Hypercar and F1 ACADEMY programmes. The deal folds SharkNinja into the team's Racing Accelerator Programme, giving the appliance maker access to McLaren's engineering process for product development, not just trackside branding.

Motorsport sponsorship has traditionally sold speed and glamour. This one sells R&D access. When a consumer brand buys into a race team's engineering process rather than a grid slot, sponsorship starts looking more like a product partnership than a media buy, and that's a much harder asset for a rival to copy than a logo.

Expect more tech and consumer brands chasing access over airtime.
